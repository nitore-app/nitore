Nitore — in English

Nitore keeps notes and tasks in one place – with photos, reminders, repeating tasks and a configurable widget. No account, no sign-in, no server: everything you write stays on your iPhone.

Questions or problems? Write to zanotti-99@proton.me. See the privacy policy for how your data is handled — the short version is that none of it ever leaves your device.
